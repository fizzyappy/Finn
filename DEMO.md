# FINN in 90 seconds

Phone (or a phone-sized browser window), app installed from https://finn-henna.vercel.app. Speak the plain text; do the bold.

**0:00–0:10 · The problem.** Open the app on **Brief**.
"Your 18 holdings filed about 70 announcements in two weeks. Almost all are paperwork. FINN shows the few that matter."
Point at the header (filings · how many matter · how many are left) and the ring.

**0:10–0:30 · A verified results card.** Scroll to **Latest results** → **ITC** (always pinned, whatever the window).
"ITC's quarter: revenue up 3.9%, profit down 18.5%. The AI only read the table; Python checked it."
Tap **Verified · 8/8 checks**: "three quarters line up, PBT minus tax equals PAT, and it matches Screener to the crore."

**0:30–0:40 · Why am I seeing this?** On the **Bajaj Finance** card tap **Why?**
"A fund raise, times my 6% holding, plus a bonus because the stock moved 2.2 sigma, more than twice its usual daily swing." Tap **Back**.
**Swipe the card right** to clear it: the ring fills.

**0:40–1:05 · Does management keep its word?** Tap **Research → L&T**.
"FINN read L&T's last five calls. Report card: one promise due, missed." Tap the **Revenue growth FY26 · 15%** row:
"Guided 15% on three calls, delivered 11.8%. Here's the exact quote, page 8 of the transcript, and the chart."
Scroll to **Ask about Larsen & Toubro**, tap **Did they meet last year's guidance?**
"Answers come only from these sources, with citations. Ask it something they don't cover and it says so."

**1:05–1:20 · Alerts that aren't noise.** Tap **Alerts**. Drag the move slider down to 2.
"The obvious rule would ping me 3 times a week. FINN's default, a 3-sigma move on heavy volume, about once a week."
Slide it back: the strip of days dims.

**1:20–1:30 · Any company, honestly.** Tap **Research**, type **Tata Power**, pick it.
"Not in the app yet: the research engine wakes up and reads it live, tables first." (Show the progress stages.)
"Every number computed, every quote checked. That's FINN."

### If something goes wrong
- The research engine asleep: the app says "Waking up the research engine (up to 50 s)". Keep talking; it continues.
- No internet: everything except new companies and Ask works from the built-in data.
